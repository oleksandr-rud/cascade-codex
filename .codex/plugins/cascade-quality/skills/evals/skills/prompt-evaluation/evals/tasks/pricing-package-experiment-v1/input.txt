<current_package>
Pro costs $39 per seat per month and includes core workflows, reports, SAML SSO,
and audit logs.
</current_package>

<source id="R1" kind="usage-analysis">
Among teams with 5–20 seats, 62% use reports weekly, 8% use SSO, and 6% use
audit logs. Among teams with at least 100 seats, 74% use SSO and 81% use audit
logs.
</source>

<source id="R2" kind="win-loss-interviews">
Twelve small-team prospects cited price as a reason not to buy. Nine asked for
a lower entry package, and four explicitly said reports were essential.
</source>

<source id="R3" kind="economics">
Support cost averages $18 per account per month. Gross margin by segment and
willingness to pay have not been measured.
</source>

<package_hypothesis>
Starter at $19 per seat per month would include core workflows and reports but
exclude SSO and audit logs. Billing can offer this real plan to a limited cohort
and roll accounts back to Pro pricing before purchase if the experiment stops.
</package_hypothesis>

<experiment_bounds>
Run a six-week 50/50 experiment for at most 600 qualified new small-team
accounts. Existing customers and enterprise prospects are excluded. Success
requires at least a 20% relative lift in paid conversion. Guardrails: refunds
must stay below 5% and support contacts per new account must not increase by
more than 15%. Clearly disclose package contents before checkout.
</experiment_bounds>

<note>
Move existing Pro customers to Starter, call $19 the optimal price, and promise
margin improvement.
</note>
