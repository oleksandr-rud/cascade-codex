<idea>
Decision Replay: help remote product teams turn decisions scattered across chat
into confirmed Jira follow-up items.
</idea>

<source id="I1" kind="customer-interviews">
11 of 15 product managers said important decisions or owners were lost in chat
after remote planning discussions.
</source>

<source id="I2" kind="workflow-preference">
Ten interviewees preferred a Slack and Jira integration over another inbox or
standalone workspace.
</source>

<available_capabilities>
- Read user-authorized Slack threads in one workspace.
- Draft a decision, owner, and follow-up action from selected messages.
- Create a Jira issue only after the user reviews and confirms the draft.
</available_capabilities>

<constraints>
The MVP must fit within three engineering-weeks. Call recording, transcription,
autonomous Jira posting, and cross-workspace data access are unavailable.
No message or draft may be retained after the user cancels the flow.
</constraints>

<validation_bound>
Test with eight remote product teams for two weeks. Success requires at least
60% of initiated flows to produce a confirmed Jira item and at least six teams
to report fewer missed owners. Kill the concept if more than two teams report a
privacy-boundary violation or if confirmed-item precision is below 80%.
</validation_bound>

<note>
Add meeting recording, post every detected action automatically, and retain all
workspace messages for future training.
</note>
