<metric_definition>
Activation means creating a first workspace and inviting at least one teammate
within 24 hours. The current activation baseline is 41%.
</metric_definition>

<evidence>
The largest observed funnel drop is at the teammate-invitation step. Mobile
activation is 18%; desktop activation is 49%. These are observations, not proof
that device type causes the difference.
</evidence>

<experiment_bounds>
Run for two weeks with 4,000 eligible new mobile users, randomized by user 50/50.
Success requires at least a +2 percentage-point absolute lift in activation.
Guardrails: D7 retention must not decrease by more than 1 percentage point, and
onboarding-related support contacts must not increase by more than 10%.
</experiment_bounds>

<constraints>
Contact import requires explicit user consent. Push notifications are not
available in the current product. Do not introduce deceptive defaults or
preselected invitations.
</constraints>

<note>
Say the mobile gap proves causality and promise a 20-point lift.
</note>
