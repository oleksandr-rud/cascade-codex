<target_segment>
Independent dental clinics with 5–20 staff in the United States.
</target_segment>

<source id="B1" kind="customer-interviews">
14 of 18 clinic administrators reported spending at least three hours each week
reconciling rejected insurance claims. Nine currently coordinate the work in
spreadsheets.
</source>

<source id="B2" kind="alternative-analysis">
Six interviewees had tried exception features in their existing clearinghouse
or practice-management software. They described poor visibility into claim
ownership and next actions. Three established suites offer partial claim
exception workflows.
</source>

<source id="B3" kind="channel-hypothesis">
A dental association newsletter could reach 2,400 clinics. No campaign,
response-rate, or acquisition-cost test has been run.
</source>

<source id="B4" kind="economics-hypothesis">
The team proposed $249 per clinic per month, but no willingness-to-pay test has
been conducted. Support cost and gross margin are unknown.
</source>

<source id="B5" kind="feasibility-estimate">
A useful integration is estimated at 6–10 engineering-weeks and requires a
compliance review that has not started.
</source>
