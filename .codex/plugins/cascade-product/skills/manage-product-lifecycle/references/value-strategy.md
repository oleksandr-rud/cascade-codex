# Value strategy and portfolio learning

Use for strategy across dependent decisions, investment allocation, or learning
that changes a prior decision. A bounded feature or market question starts at
its owning skill; do not run every stage or create a graph by default.

Freeze objective, actors, geography, scope/exclusions, stage, evidence horizon,
capacity, and commitment at risk. Current scope outranks historical examples.
Separate evidence strength, attractiveness, approval, and readiness.

Follow the uncertain dependencies: desire/job -> market -> reason to choose ->
delivery mechanism -> useful outcome -> offer -> features -> growth -> learning.
This is a reasoning map, not a mandatory phase sequence. Use existing Product
decision IDs, work-product traceability and source bindings for lineage.
A changed brief can retire a bet without disproving its market evidence.

Use cascade-market:research-market for fresh sources,
cascade-market:evaluate-market-opportunity for comparative market selection,
cascade-market:brand-positioning for a reason to choose, and
cascade-market:plan-growth for channel and lifecycle growth decisions.
Use cascade-product:define-product for value, offer and feature proposals and
cascade-product:validate-product for outcome evidence. Keep scheduling and
delivery coordination with cascade-project-management:plan-project.

## Allocation

For multiple bets, compare the same objective, horizon, capacity and downside.
Normalize each by actor, job, outcome, market, mechanism, offer, channel, evidence,
dependencies, and next uncertainty. Apply hard eligibility gates before ranking.
When coverage differs, allocate to decisive tests rather than inventing a winner.

Consider learning value, upside/downside, reversibility, time to evidence,
capability fit, and correlated assumptions. Name concentration in customers,
channels, geography, platforms, data, providers, or key people. More nominal
bets do not diversify a shared failure mode. Reusable infrastructure is an
advantage only when it improves delivery, cost, quality, or evidence.

Propose the next unit of capacity, owner, time/cost ceiling, review trigger and
stop rule. Include failed bets, abandoned cohorts, spend, and founder/staff time
when assessing a repeatable strategy. Learning alone cannot justify indefinite
investment; keep its milestone and exposure ceiling explicit.

## Re-entry

When evidence changes, identify the earliest challenged assumption and its
dependent requirements, experiments and delivery slices. Invalidate or mark
those consumers provisional; keep unrelated decisions current. A generic
"implements" relation is insufficient when an upstream premise is necessary:
record that dependency explicitly in the decision rationale and traceability.

Distinguish increase, continue, hold, reduce, pause, split, or stop as proposals
under the existing owner-held gates. Retain conflicting evidence and the result
that would reverse the recommendation. More data, engagement or revenue is not
proof of realized outcomes, sustainable margin, or compounding advantage.
