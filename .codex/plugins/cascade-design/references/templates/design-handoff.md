# Single brief and design handoff

This is a compact information template, not a new mandatory file/schema. Reuse
existing target design notes. Omit irrelevant sections and avoid duplicate prose.
For typed delivery use the existing Design review schema/coverage variant.

## Grounded brief and decisions

- Actor/job/context, surface/entry, useful completion and non-goals.
- Governing product, design, brand and implementation observations: identity,
  revision, authority and any conflict.
- Actual platform, viewports/states, constraints, tool/format and authorized paths.
- Selected direction and settled behavior: actions, controls/units, persistence,
  unsaved work, interruptions/recovery, responsive and accessibility expectations.
- Assumptions and consequential unresolved decisions, with owner/dependency.

## Exact artifact index

| Frame ID | Revision | Viewport/state | Editable locator | Preview locator | Capture conditions | Inspection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Use actual identities | Bound source revision | A declared required pair | Existing editable source | Actual viewable artifact | Theme/locale/content/fonts/DPR as relevant | Actual result or gap |

Every required pair needs an inspected frame for creation READY. A future path
or shared responsive rule is not a frame. Describe prototype-only operations and
fixture data explicitly. Bind assets/tokens and licenses/fallbacks where relevant.

## Review and next owner

- Findings: exact source/expected/observed/consequence/severity/owner.
- Evidence plan: check, actual receipt/status or what remains to run.
- Deviations: only those actually approved/authorized, with their source.
- Implementation handoff: accepted reference/version, required pairs, interaction/
  responsive rules, assets/tokens and fidelity checks. Host acceptance is separate.
- Review evidence preparation: actual preview/capture view for design-evidence or
  visual-evidence, preserving original source/frame IDs and locators.
- Remaining gaps/blocks, bounded recovery attempted, exact next action/owner.

The requesting host owns persistence, artifact access, production implementation
and acceptance. Do not add unsupported JSON fields, claim approval from silence,
or list a missing desired future output as a supplied source/input.
