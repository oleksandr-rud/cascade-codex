# Experience value

Use when reviewing a proposed or current journey's useful outcome and burden.
Work within supplied product intent; propose a product delta when the outcome
or mechanism needs to change.

Map before, during and after: trigger/promise -> understanding/action ->
confirmation/progress -> recovery/closure. Locate the dominant cost: finding,
understanding, deciding, starting, entering information, doing, waiting,
correcting, or knowing whether the job is complete. Separate observed friction
from inferred friction.

Compare direct control, retrieval, guidance, coaching/practice, delegated action,
voice and human support. Conversation is not automatically best. Remove
unnecessary effort while retaining informed choice, accuracy, learning,
accessibility, confirmation and recovery. Count staff review and exceptions as
experience costs.

Add memory, personalization or proactive help only when it improves an identified
decision or outcome. Make inferred context correctable. Match support cadence
to a finite, episodic, recurring or learning job; quiet completion and increased
independence can be better than repeated engagement.

Compare task success, active effort, elapsed time, correction, understanding and
actual outcome against the existing journey. A designed flow is not a tested
flow. Return the affected Product requirement/section IDs, revised path, retained
and removed friction, failure/recovery, and the smallest useful check through
the existing design-review findings, evidence plan and handoff fields.
