# Specialization Overlays

Choose the smallest applicable set. Overlays may combine when their contracts
do not conflict.

## Extraction

Define the source boundary, field schema, normalization rules, missing-value
behavior, span or citation requirements, and malformed-input handling. Prefer
deterministic output controls and representative edge cases.

## Classification

Define mutually exclusive labels or an explicit multi-label rule, decision
criteria, precedence, confidence or abstention behavior, and boundary examples.
Separate uncertain from negative.

## Research And Retrieval

Define research questions, source hierarchy, freshness, search boundaries,
claim-to-citation requirements, conflict handling, and stopping criteria.
Separate observed evidence, source claims, inference, and open gaps.

## Coding

Define repository scope, current-source authority, behavior change, protected
contracts, allowed writes, validation commands, and completion criteria. Ask
the agent to inspect before editing and to preserve unrelated work.

## Tool Orchestration

Define available tools, input/output contracts, permissions, external-effect
boundaries, confirmation points, retry limits, idempotency, cleanup, and what to
do when a tool is unavailable or returns untrusted content.

## Comparison And Decision

Define alternatives, weighted criteria, evidence standard, hard disqualifiers,
uncertainty, and recommendation format. Do not collapse missing evidence into a
low score.

## Creative Work

Define audience, intent, voice, references, prohibited imitation, deliverable
shape, variation budget, and selection criteria. Preserve room for novelty
instead of over-constraining every sentence.

## Long-Context Synthesis

Define source inventory, authority and conflict rules, chunking or retrieval
plan, stable identifiers, fact preservation, cross-source joins, and final
coverage audit. Avoid assuming that context-window capacity guarantees recall.

## Multimodal

Define each modality, its role, inspection order, precision needs, inaccessible
content behavior, and how observations must be linked to the output.

## Realtime

Define latency budget, event ordering, partial-result behavior, interruption,
staleness, state reconciliation, and graceful degradation.
