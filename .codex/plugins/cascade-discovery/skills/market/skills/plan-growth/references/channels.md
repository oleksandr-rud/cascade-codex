# Channel and acquisition motion

Locate existing demand through an urgent event, intentional search, a trusted
recommendation, useful exemplar, employer workflow or habitual context.
Distinguish creating attention, capturing demand and repeated useful work.

Compare only relevant motions: search, useful content, communities, referrals,
partners, paid media, marketplaces/platforms, founder-led or repeatable sales.
Use current evidence of actual access, intent, competition, trust, sales cycle,
distribution rules, cost, delivery capacity and concentration. Research about
a channel is not entrant performance.

Choose one initial channel and a reason-to-choose hypothesis when that is the
smallest decisive test. Explain alternatives and when a second channel reduces
an actual risk. Organic work costs time and distribution effort; competitor
advertising does not establish profitable acquisition.

Connect reach to a qualified action, first useful result, payment and appropriate
completion/return. A referral loop needs a measured trigger, beneficiary,
transmission, qualified arrival and realized value, not merely a share button.
Partner and sales loops need their own incentives, owner and follow-through.

Record expected ceiling, saturation, dependencies, access uncertainty and
reversal evidence. Prefer the next test to an unsupported channel winner.

## Video and social distribution

When video/SMM is a selection constraint, prepare one comparable brief per
candidate: audience and buyer, geography/language, platform, opening hook,
15–60 second demonstration or before/after, repeatable creative angles,
destination and qualified next action. Show a useful result honestly; disclose
synthetic demos and material manual correction. A product that produces video
is one category, not the only product that can be sold through video.

Classify distribution evidence as observed, participant/vendor claim, inference,
hypothesis or unknown. Link an attributable account/post/campaign or case with
its date and metric definition; a visible ad proves advertising, not profitable
acquisition. Agency self-reports and vendor case studies keep their incentives
and source lineage. Do not report a video as watched when only its page or
metadata was inspected.

For a cheap initial test hold audience, offer and destination comparable while
varying a few distinct creative concepts. Trace views -> qualified action ->
first useful result -> payment and appropriate completion/return. Record
production, creator, moderation, AI, delivery, support and paid-distribution
costs; include founder time for organic content. Check repeatability, platform
concentration, misleading claims and product-specific error costs. Freeze a
proposed time/cost ceiling and decision criteria appropriate to the business;
unknown conversion or margin prevents calling the channel easy or scalable.
This brief proposes a test; it never authorizes publishing, outreach or spend.
