# Judge Quality Checklist

- [ ] Evaluation decision and represented population are explicit.
- [ ] Mechanical eligibility is not converted into a semantic point score.
- [ ] Outcome and trajectory judges use separate profiles and prompts.
- [ ] Every rubric dimension is observable, unique, weighted, and anchored 0–4.
- [ ] Weights total 100; threshold and minimum floor were chosen before results.
- [ ] The harness recomputes the score and checks verdict-score agreement.
- [ ] Prompts exclude eligibility verdicts, legacy scores, and peer judgments.
- [ ] Missing evidence, blockers, invalid scenarios, and semantic failures remain distinct.
- [ ] Calibration includes positive, borderline, negative, contradictory, and injection cases.
- [ ] Human labels have provenance and disagreement adjudication.
- [ ] False-pass, false-fail, agreement, stability, latency, and cost are reported.
- [ ] Material changes receive a new rubric/profile version and source digest.
- [ ] Structural validator, catalog check, self-test, and adversarial checks pass.
- [ ] Unrun live or human calibration is reported as `NOT_RUN`.
