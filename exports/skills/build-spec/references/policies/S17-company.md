# S17 — Company operating model

Describe customers, offerings, responsibilities, processes, partners, information
ownership and outcome measures. Separate current operations from proposed changes.
Specify handoff triggers, accountable roles, deliverables, exceptions and escalation.
Business roles are not software classes or autonomous agent assignments.

The section contains an operating model and responsibility records. Its handoff
or swimlane diagram shows who does what and what information/result passes to
the next role. Bind nodes to named responsibilities and outputs to acceptance.
Use formal BPMN only when its exact event/gateway semantics are needed and reviewed.

Check unowned work and failure handoffs. See
[company interaction guidance](../../../spec-diagrams/references/interaction-company.md).
