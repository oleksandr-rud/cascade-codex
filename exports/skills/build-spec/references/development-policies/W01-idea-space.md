# W01 — Expand ideas, then choose a direction

Use after understanding the user outcome through S01. The question might concern
a whole product, a capability, an interaction or a way to enforce a rule. State
the decision, desired benefit and known constraints. Do not infer a business
priority or desired feature merely from a familiar product category.

## Generate before judging

Choose one useful expansion move for this question: a dimension-by-option table,
an analogy from another domain, an assumption reversal, or a counterexample to
the current idea. Explain the move briefly. Generate mechanisms that actually
change the experience or implementation. Preserve an unusual plausible option
long enough to understand it; a rename is not another solution.

For each candidate describe an actor doing something, the resulting value, the
needed information/dependencies and a likely failure or trade-off. Treat demand,
usability and feasibility as hypotheses until supported. There is no idea quota
and no requirement to search externally merely because brainstorming is active.

## Converge against the user's purpose

Once options are useful, compare them against the actual constraints, benefit,
effort, reversibility and uncertainty. Pick only criteria that change this decision;
do not manufacture weighted scores. Recommend a direction and state why the
strongest alternative loses under the current assumptions.

A consequential unresolved product preference belongs to the owner. A routine
reversible detail within the task can be decided with a reason. Silence is not
acceptance. If evidence is needed, give W02 a narrow question; if a mechanism
needs engineering detail, give W03 that decision.

## Capture only what the next stage needs

Record the selected or proposed direction, useful alternatives, governing
assumptions and remaining questions in S01/S02/S03 or the conversation. Keep
source-backed facts separate from hypotheses. A sketch or scenario comparison
can explain the difference; reuse the relevant spec diagram when possible.
Do not carry the entire brainstorm into persistent implementation instructions.
